## What changed in the tree

- `loader/data-regions.h`: only `darwin_var` (write_first 0, write_end 8388608). A's super line is retired.
- `loader/build-standalone.sh`: `DATAVOL_PART` (default `darwin_var`), checked against `data-regions.h`.
- `loader/oem-bootos.patch`: target `pmos-shell` (5). `tools/bootos.sh pmos-shell`.
- `userland-26/scripts/mkimage-trim.sh`: FULL is bounded by super (9,126,805,504 B), padded to 32 MiB only.
- `tools/flash-root.sh`: writes the image's own size (a whole number of MiB, at most super), hashes darwin_var's first
  64 MiB before and after. `tools/var-write.sh`: option B constants. `tools/metal-cache-seed.sh`: reads darwin_var.
- `tools/run-flashroot.sh`, `wifi-run.sh`, `kernel/drivers/gpu/gpu-run.sh`, `gpu-quietrun.sh` (`UFS` overridable):
  `ufs_data=darwin_var`.
- `tools/pmos-debug-shell.py` (telnet into pmOS's initramfs shell), `tools/planb-session.sh` (the one-session shrink +
  carve, as run), `tools/planb-gpt-rehearsal.sh` (the sgdisk rehearsal on a sparse image).
- `docs/data-volume.md` section 7 "Status (2026-11-12)": the whole procedure and its pitfalls (the initramfs regrows
  p2 and the ext4 on every boot until the outer table is smaller: everything has to happen in ONE debug-shell
  session; Linux and U-Boot reject a primary GPT whose last usable LBA is past the device, so the inner table is
  rewritten for the new size BEFORE the outer shrink).
- `u-boot-lisa`: `boot/pxe_utils.c` (pxe_append), `board/qualcomm/hakios-lisa.env` (pmos_normal, pmos_shell,
  osel_5, the menu renumbered, `bootmenu_default 2`), `build.sh`, `README.md`.
- The sysroot `userland-26/out/sysroot-26` is the COMPLETE one now (`APPLE_ONLY=keep` restore + all metallibs) with
  the glass2 plug-in. A root for the old 4608 MiB region needs `prune-sysroot.py --apply` first.
